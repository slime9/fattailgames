About

Small studio, deliberate craft

Fat Tail Games, LLC is an independent video game development studio based in Irvine, California. Founded in 2026, we design and build original video game experiences for players, with a focus on craftsmanship and thoughtful design over volume.

As a small, founder-led studio, every decision — mechanical, visual, narrative — passes through the same hands. That's a constraint we've chosen on purpose.

Focus

What we're building

We specialize in video game design, development, and publishing across digital platforms. Our current work is in early development, with more details to be announced as our first project progresses.
